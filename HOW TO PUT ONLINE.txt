FREELANCE BOOKS - SHARE IT AS A LINK WITH NGROK (FREE)
======================================================

Do this once, on the computer that keeps the books (the office PC).
The link only works while that computer is on and start-online.bat is running.

1. MAKE A FREE NGROK ACCOUNT
   - Go to https://ngrok.com and sign up (free plan).

2. GET YOUR FIXED FREE ADDRESS
   - In the ngrok dashboard open  Domains  (Universal Gateway > Domains).
   - Free accounts get one fixed address, something like
        apex-books.ngrok-free.app
     If none is listed, click "New Domain" / "Create Domain" to claim your free one.
   - Copy it.

3. INSTALL NGROK
   - Easiest: open "Command Prompt" and run:
        winget install ngrok.ngrok
     (or download it from https://ngrok.com/download and follow their steps)

4. CONNECT NGROK TO YOUR ACCOUNT
   - In the dashboard open  Your Authtoken  and copy the command shown, which looks like:
        ngrok config add-authtoken 2abc...your-token...
   - Paste it into Command Prompt and press Enter.
   - Keep that token private. Do not send it to anyone.

5. PUT YOUR ADDRESS IN THE SETTINGS FILE
   - In this folder open the file  .env  with Notepad.
   - On the line  NGROK_DOMAIN=  add your address, with no https:// in front:
        NGROK_DOMAIN=apex-books.ngrok-free.app
   - Save.

6. GO ONLINE
   - Double-click  start-online.bat
   - It shows the link, for example  https://apex-books.ngrok-free.app
   - Send that link to whoever needs it. They sign in with:
        User name: vinitha
        Password:  password123
   - The first time someone opens the link, ngrok shows a short
     "You are about to visit..." page. Click "Visit Site". That is normal on the free plan.

EVERY DAY
- Double-click start-online.bat. Keep its windows open (two run minimised).
- The link is the same every time.
- Close the windows to take the books offline. Everything is already saved.

CHANGING THE LOGIN
- Edit BOOKS_USER and BOOKS_PASSWORD in .env, save, then close and restart start-online.bat.
- Please choose a stronger password than password123 before sharing the link widely:
  anyone on the internet can reach the login page.

KEEP A BACKUP
- Once a week: Business & Tools -> Backup & Restore -> Export backup.
  Keep that file on Google Drive, OneDrive or a pen drive.
